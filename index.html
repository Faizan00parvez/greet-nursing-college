Why GREET

A serious place to train as a nurse

Nursing is a regulated profession — where you train matters. GREET Nursing College runs recognised programmes under the oversight of the Indian Nursing Council, the state nursing council, and a state university.

  • Recognised programmes — recognised by INC (New Delhi), approved by JNRC, affiliated to Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag
  • Clinical exposure — nursing training anchored in real hospital and community-health practice
  • Placement assistance — guidance into nursing roles after you qualify
  • Scholarship available — talk to the college office about what you may be eligible for
