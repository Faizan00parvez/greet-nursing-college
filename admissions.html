Documents — typically required

Please note: this is the list usually asked for — confirm the exact requirement with the college office before your visit.

  • Class 10 marksheet and certificate
  • Class 12 (Intermediate) marksheet and certificate
  • GNM marksheet and certificate (for Post Basic B.Sc Nursing applicants)
  • Transfer / migration certificate
  • Photo ID proof (Aadhaar, etc.)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Caste, income and domicile certificates — where applicable

Scholarship

Scholarship is available — eligibility depends on the scheme and the student. Government scholarship schemes such as e-Kalyan may apply for eligible students. Ask the college office what you qualify for; they'll guide you through the paperwork.
