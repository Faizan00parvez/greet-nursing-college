The Institution

GREET Nursing College, Pachrukhi

GREET Nursing College is part of the GREET Group of Institutions at Pachrukhi, Rajdhanwar (Giridih, Jharkhand) — the same campus and the same team behind Greet Teacher Training College, which has trained teachers for the region since 2007.

The nursing college offers four recognised programmes — ANM, GNM, B.Sc Nursing and Post Basic B.Sc Nursing — carrying the group's focus on disciplined, affordable, career-oriented education into healthcare.

Recognition & affiliation

The college's programmes are recognised by the Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi, approved by the Jharkhand Nurses Registration Council (JNRC), and affiliated to Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag — the three credentials that matter for a nursing qualification in Jharkhand.
