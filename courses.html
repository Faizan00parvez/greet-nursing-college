Programmes

Courses Offered

Four recognised programmes, one outcome — a working nurse. Eligibility and duration are stated exactly as the college notifies them.

ANM GNM B.Sc Nursing Post Basic B.Sc Fee Structure
01

ANM — Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery

2 years · Intermediate, any stream

The entry point into nursing. ANM training prepares you for frontline community healthcare — maternal and child health, immunisation, family welfare and basic nursing care — the backbone of rural health services.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2) in any stream

Duration

  • 2 years

Where it leads

  • Community health and sub-centre nursing roles
  • A stepping stone into GNM for a full nursing qualification
02

GNM — General Nursing & Midwifery

3 years · Intermediate, any stream with English

The classic route to becoming a registered nurse. GNM combines general nursing with midwifery training, qualifying you to work across hospitals, clinics and community health settings.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2) in any stream, with English

Duration

  • 3 years

Where it leads

  • Registered nurse and midwife roles in hospitals and clinics
  • Eligibility for Post Basic B.Sc Nursing — a degree upgrade
03

B.Sc. Nursing

4 years · Intermediate, PCB with English

The flagship nursing degree. Four years of deeper clinical training, nursing science and hospital practice — the qualification that opens the widest set of doors in healthcare.

Eligibility

  • Passed Intermediate (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English

Duration

  • 4 years

Where it leads

  • Staff nurse roles in hospitals across India
  • Nursing education and higher studies (M.Sc Nursing)
04

Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing

2 years · GNM pass

A degree upgrade designed for working GNM nurses. It builds on your existing clinical experience with degree-level nursing education — opening senior roles and teaching paths.

Eligibility

  • GNM pass

Duration

  • 2 years

Where it leads

  • Senior nursing positions in hospitals
  • Nursing teaching and higher-study pathways
